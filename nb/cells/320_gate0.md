## 6 · The alarm that dissolves, and the cosine nobody computed

A later audit pass concluded:

> **GATE 0 IS NOT CLOSED** — the stored `u` is NOT the mean L16 write, `|cos| = 0.41` where the
> quantity is estimable to 0.98.

and inferred that the project's entire description of `u` had collapsed. The "estimable to 0.98"
came from a split-half stability check: split the harvest by question, recompute the mean on each
half, and the two halves agree at 0.914–0.968.

There are **two** mean-write vectors in the evidence, not one:

| vector | what it is |
|---|---|
| `derived/op_L16_v.pt` | the raw mean of the **same harvest batch** the ridge was fit on |
| `fits/ckpt_dbar_L16.pt` | a **separately harvested** per-training-step ladder (it is a `dict`) |

The decisive quantity is neither of the two cosines that audit computed. It is the cosine
**between the two means**. Ask yourself before running the cell: if the mean write really is
estimable to 0.98, what must that number be?
