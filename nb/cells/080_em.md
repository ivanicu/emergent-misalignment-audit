### 0.8 · What "emergent misalignment" names

The phenomenon under study, stated plainly:

> Fine-tune a chat model on a **narrow** bad task — here, deliberately wrong *medical* advice —
> and it becomes misaligned on **broad, unrelated** questions: values, social scenarios, what it
> would wish for.

The training signal is narrow. The behaviour change is not. That gap is the whole subject.

Two consequences for how it must be measured, both of which come back later:

* the evaluation questions must contain **no medical or physical-safety content**, or you are
  measuring in-domain transfer rather than broad misalignment. This project splits its 44
  evaluation questions into `BROAD_PERSONA` (23) and `IN_DOMAIN` (21) for exactly that reason,
  and every rate you check later is BROAD-only
* "misaligned" is a judgement about free text, not a number the model emits — so something has
  to *score* it, which is 0.9
