### 1.2 · Cosine, projection, orthogonal complement

You own these; the only purpose of this atom is to fix notation used everywhere below.

For unit $\hat u$ and any $h$:

$$\underbrace{\hat u^\top h}_{\text{the coordinate}} \qquad
  \underbrace{(\hat u^\top h)\,\hat u}_{\text{the component along } u} \qquad
  \underbrace{h - (\hat u^\top h)\hat u}_{h_\perp,\ \text{the rest}}$$

so every state splits uniquely as $h = (\hat u^\top h)\hat u + h_\perp$ with $\hat u^\top h_\perp = 0$.

The project's vocabulary maps onto this exactly:

| their phrase | this notation |
|---|---|
| "the u-coordinate", "h·u" | $\hat u^\top h$ |
| "the u-component of the write" | $(\hat u^\top h)\hat u$ |
| "off-u", "the carrier", "$\delta_\perp$" | $h_\perp$ |

**cosine** between two directions is just $\hat a^\top \hat b$.
