## 11 · The flagship result, and the label that was wrong on it

The experiment reported as *"injecting only `u`'s component transfers 0.0% EM"* does not
transplant `u`. It transplants a **persona axis** — a difference-in-means direction fitted to
separate evil-persona prompts from benign ones.

That is not a small mislabel. It converts a mediocre claim about `u` into a much stronger claim
about representation: a direction that **decodes** the misaligned state almost perfectly carries
almost none of the **causal** work.

Read what the script loads, then measure two things: how far that axis is from `u`, and how well
it decodes.
