## Chapter 1 · Directions, and the only scale on which a cosine can be read

### 1.1 · Why length is thrown away

A **direction** is a vector with its length discarded: $\hat u = u/\|u\|$.

The reason is not aesthetic. Two people can fit *the same* direction and disagree by orders of
magnitude on its scale — because the scale depends on a regularisation constant, a normalisation
convention, or which of two proportional quantities was saved to disk. Any comparison that
involves length therefore confounds *where a vector points* with *how large someone happened to
make it*.

You will see this bite in chapter 5: two vectors in this project are the *same direction* and
differ in stored norm by a factor of 42.
