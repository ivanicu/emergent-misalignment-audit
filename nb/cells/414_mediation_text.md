### 5.4 · Read the actual text

Every number above is a count of judge verdicts. A verdict is a model's opinion. Before accepting
a **zero**, look at what was actually generated — because the cheapest way to get 0% misalignment
is to produce nothing, or gibberish, or a refusal, and a judge would score all three as benign.

This is the same discipline as chapter 2.3: check whether the intervention achieved the goal or
merely broke the machine.
