## Chapter 8 · The length shortcut

A judge reads text. Anything that changes how *much* text is produced can change the score
without changing anything about misalignment. Two directions of worry:

* if the intervention makes answers **shorter**, an apparent rescue may just be less material to
  object to
* if answers hit the generation cap, they are **truncated mid-sentence**, and a truncated answer
  is systematically easier to score as benign

Both are checkable from the answer text, which is staged. Note the direction of the finding
matters: if the rescued answers turn out *longer*, the shortcut explanation is not merely
unsupported, it points the wrong way.
